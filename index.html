01 / ABOUT

Technology should solve real problems.

I'm Joel Damilare, the person behind TechyJojo. I work in AI automation and bring together a background in Electrical and Electronics Engineering, creative design, customer service and technology.

TechyJojo is where I share my journey through technology: learning, experimenting, building practical systems and explaining what I discover. Over time, my interest in how technology can improve the way people work grew, leading me to explore workflow automation, artificial intelligence and digital business solutions.

With an academic background in Electrical and Electronics Engineering, specialising in Instrumentation and Control, I developed an interest in systems, processes and how different components work together. Today, I bring that same mindset into building automated workflows and AI-powered solutions.

I work with tools and technologies such as n8n, Python, JavaScript, APIs, webhooks, Google Sheets and AI/LLM integrations to develop solutions that help businesses reduce repetitive tasks, manage customer interactions and improve operational efficiency.

I'm particularly interested in building practical technology solutions that solve real-world problems, improve business processes and create better experiences for people.

My goal is simple: to use technology to make work smarter, businesses more efficient and everyday processes easier.
